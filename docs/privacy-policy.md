# PulseBoard Privacy Policy (placeholder)

> Have this reviewed before App Store submission. You also need to host it at a public URL: set `PRIVACY_POLICY_URL` in `ios/Config/AppConfig.xcconfig` and add the same URL in App Store Connect. The web app serves the same text at `/privacy.html`.

- **No servers.** PulseBoard has no servers. We do not collect, upload, sell or share your health data. We never use it for advertising or marketing.
- **iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch**
  - Health data is read from Apple Health and processed on your device.
  - Entries you log are saved to Apple Health, which syncs them under Apple's privacy policy.
  - Only non-health settings sync through your private iCloud: goals, layouts, saved meals, preferred sources and challenge setup. We can't access that data.
  - Custom metrics stay on the device.
- **Web**
  - Your Apple Health export is processed entirely in your browser and stored in that browser's IndexedDB.
  - It is never uploaded, and you can delete it at any time in Settings.
- **No tracking.** No analytics, advertising or third-party SDKs.
- **Challenges (preview).** If you opt in for a specific challenge, only one aggregated daily score would be shared, never raw health data. Local challenges use your city or region only.
- **AI insights** run on-device using Apple Intelligence. No data leaves your device.
- **Deleting your data.** Delete the app, or use "Delete all data" on the web. Your Apple Health data itself is managed in the Health app.

Summary

  • PulseBoard has no servers. We do not collect, upload, sell or share your health data, and never use it for advertising or marketing.
  • iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch: health data is read from Apple Health and processed on your device. Entries you log are saved to Apple Health, which syncs them under Apple's privacy policy. Only non-health settings (goals, layouts, saved meals, preferred sources, challenge setup) sync through your private iCloud, which we cannot access. Custom metrics stay on the device.
  • Web: your Apple Health export is processed entirely in your browser and stored in this browser's local storage (IndexedDB). It is never uploaded. You can delete it at any time in Settings.
  • No analytics, advertising or third-party SDKs.
  • Challenges (preview): if you opt in for a specific challenge, only one aggregated daily score would be shared — never raw health data. Local challenges use your city/region only.
  • AI insights run on-device using Apple Intelligence; no data leaves your device.
